Southern White Gravy Chicken Sandwich

DifficultyHard
Cook Time1 hr+
HeatMedium 🌶🌶
Components8 components
Servings
2

Down-South biscuits and gravy vibes on a bun — thick, peppery, creamy white sausage gravy smothered over crispy fried chicken. A buttermilk slaw cuts through the richness, a sage and sausage bacon jam brings that breakfast flavor, and a roasted garlic goat cheese spread holds the bottom bun together. Fried onion strings on top for crunch. Sunday morning comfort in every bite.
