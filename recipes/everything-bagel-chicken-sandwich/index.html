Everything Bagel Chicken Sandwich

DifficultyHard
Cook Time1 hr+
HeatNone
Components8 components
Servings
2

A bold, savory breakfast-inspired sandwich that transforms a breakfast bagel concept into a loaded chicken creation. Crispy fried chicken topped with a creamy everything bagel sauce made from mayo, sour cream, and cream cheese blended with everything bagel seasoning sits atop a bed of lox-style pickled red onions, ham, crispy bacon, cheddar cheese, and a fried egg. The everything bagel slaw provides a crunchy base, while fried tomato and fresh dill add brightness to this indulgent, over-the-top sandwich.
