Whipped Feta Spread Chicken Sandwich

DifficultyHard
Cook Time1 hr+
HeatMedium 🌶🌶
Components8 components
Servings
2

Greek mezze plate on a bun — tangy whipped feta with lemon, garlic, and red pepper flakes gets schmeared on the bottom bun under crispy fried chicken. Roasted red peppers, Kalamata olive tapenade, and fresh cucumber pile on top. A Greek slaw with feta and olives adds crunch, a sun-dried tomato olive bacon jam and the whipped feta spread load up the bottom bun. Pita chips for crunch. It's like ordering everything at a Greek restaurant and putting it on fried chicken.
