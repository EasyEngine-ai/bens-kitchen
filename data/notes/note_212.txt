BBQ Candied Party Ribs
---
2 Racks of Baby Back Ribs Chopped Into Singles

Coat them with Yellow mustard, Hot Sauce and BBQ rub

Smoke them 

Take them off and cover them with BBQ Sauce and Brown Sugar and then Put into an Aluminum Tin with Butter, Jalapeños, Pineapples or Apples and more BBQ Sauce and Brown Sugar and cook them for another 2 hours

Take them off and cover them with crushed Jalapeño Kettle Chips
