Bussin Breakfast Burrito


Mix 3 eggs, Mayo, butter, jalapenos powder into a bowl, pour onto a plate then put plate into microwave and microwave for 50 seconds. 

Sautéed onions, peppers, garlic and green chiles, corn

Roasted potatoes or crispy fries

Pork belly in hot honey

Goat cheese, pepper jack, and American cheese, chojta 

Mix Chalua, chipotle adobo, cilantro and Sour cream to make the sauce 

Cook 3 egg yolks only and cook them until runny then put them on burrito and wrap that shit up

Optional: salsa and guacamole
