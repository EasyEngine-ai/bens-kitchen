---
Creamy Greek Lemon Herb Potatoes 

Yukon Gold potatoes (roasted or boiled in chicken stock)
Olive oil
Fresh lemon juice lots of it
Bit of sour cream or Greek yogurt 
Chicken broth 
Garlic
Red onions
Rosemary 
Thyme
Italian seasoning 
Chicken bullion 
Salt
Black pepper
Optional: Zatar seasoning 

Best: serve as side with chicken thighs and use the fat from the chicken to mix into the potatoes
