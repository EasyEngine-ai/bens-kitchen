Crazy Chicken Sandwiches
---
ALL SANDWICHES USE CHICKEN THIGHS AND HAVE THEIR OWN SPECIAL  TOPPINGS that will go well with the theme of the sandwich and also complement the flavors and mesh well with the idea of the sandwich, be creative and think both inside the box for classic or known toppings like coleslaws, pickles, onions, tomatoes, and lettuce and also out side of the box for cool an creative ideas from YouTube chefs and other creative cooks for inspiration.  condiments as well if needed AND ALWAYS FRIED IN TALLOW OR LARD, WET AND DRY BATTER SEASONED WITH CHICKEN BUION AND OTHER SPICES BASED ON EACH SANDWICH ON BUTTERED TOASTED YELLOW POTATO BUNS or Brioche Buns
 for example:   Butter Chicken Sandwich

Fry Chicken in lard with curry seasoned breading mix of half flour and half potato starch
Cover it in Butter Chicken sauce Top with:
Picked red onions super thin
Tomatoes
Goat Cheese
Mint Cucumber Lassi for sauce
Potato Buns
