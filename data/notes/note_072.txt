Candied Bacon, Jalapeños, and Apples Burger Topping 

Bacon
jalapeños
Red apples
Raw sugar
Maple Syrup 
Apple cider vinegar
Red pepper flakes

Method: Bring sugar, vinegar, and pepper flakes to a boil. Add jalapeños and simmer 5 minutes. Cool. Store in a jar. Sweet, spicy, crunchy — the perfect burger topper.
