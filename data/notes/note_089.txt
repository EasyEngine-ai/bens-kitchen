Lemon Rosemary Chicken Rice

Ingredients:
• Chicken thighs (skin-on)
• Basmati rice
• Chicken stock & Lemon juice
• Yellow onion, garlic, and red peppers
• Rosemary, parsley, and mint
• Lemon pepper
• For serving: Sour cream and pickled red onions

Directions:
1. Cook Chicken: Sear thighs in a pan until done. Save the chicken grease in a cup.
2. Cook Rice: Use the chicken grease and lemon juice as the cooking liquid for the Basmati rice.
3. Prep Chicken: Shred or chop the cooked thighs (keep the skin on).
4. Deglaze: Pour chicken stock into the chicken pan. Add diced onion, garlic, and red peppers to sauté and deglaze the brown bits.
5. Combine: Fold the cooked rice and chopped chicken into the pan with the veggies and broth.
6. Season: Add lemon pepper, extra lemon juice, plenty of rosemary, parsley, and mint.
7. Finish: Simmer until the broth is absorbed. Serve with sour cream and pickled red onions.
