Tomato Jam burger topping 

             Roma tomatoes
	∙	Brown sugar
	∙	Apple cider vinegar
	∙	Fresh ginger
	∙	Cumin
	∙	Smoked paprika
	∙	Cayenne
	∙	Salt
Chop tomatoes and combine everything in a heavy pot. Bring to a boil, reduce heat, and simmer stirring often until thick and jammy. Works as a grown-up ketchup replacement.
