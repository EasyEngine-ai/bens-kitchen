Burger Sauces

Red sauce-
Ketchup
Mayonnaise
Small Squirt of Yellow Mustard
Werstishire
Paprika
Salt and Pepper
Saute Garlic Sweet Onions in Bacon Fat Until Slightly Crispy (Option: Add Red Bell Pepper or Poblano Pepper into Saute)
Chop up pickles into small chunks (or us Dill Relish is lazy) then mix everything together
(Option: Sweet Relish instead of pickles)
(Option: Slash of Red Wine Vinegar)

Yellow Sauce-
Yellow Mustard
Mayonnaise
Salt and Pepper
Paprika
(Option: Add Honey)
Saute Garlic Sweet Onions in Bacon Fat Until Slightly Crispy
Chop Pickles into small chunks (or us Dill Relish is lazy) then mix everything together
(Option: Sweet Relish instead of pickles)
(Option: Splash of White or Apple Cider Vinegar)
