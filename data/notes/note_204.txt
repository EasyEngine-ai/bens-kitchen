Pork Belly Chicharron with Guacamole
---
Pork Belly covered in buffalo sauce and BBQ rub then sliced in 4in sections and cut slits half way through the belly every 1in then smoked for 2 hours 

After smoking fry in lard till crispy 

Cover in chilli lime or carne asada seasoning and then dip it in fresh guacamole 

Guacamole:
Avacado 
Cilantro 
Tomatos 
Red Onion
Garlic salt
Black pepper 
Lime and lemon juice
