This Honda civic model is widely regarded as one of the most reliable cars ever made and this one only has 90k miles on the original motor. That means this car is still bulletproof and will be for the next 10 years or more. I am a mechanic who has solely bought and sold Honda civics for the last 8 years because of one reason, they hold their value. I bought this car from my friend at 55k miles after he bought it from an elderly woman who literally only drove it to church and to the grocery store.
Since then I used it as a back up care and how to teach my nephew how to work on cars. Because of that this car has

NEW:

Rotors
Front Suspension 
Battery 
Tires
Head Lights
Tail lights 
Alternator 
Belts

Paint job
Rear racing spoiler 
Legal Window Tint

What is could use but doesn’t need: 
New Power steering pump (part cost $100 and a 10yo could replace with 2 wrenches and a YouTube video in 20 min)

I also understand the value of maintenance on a car so it has had regular oil changes and transmission fluid changes as well as only premium gas run through it will regular treatments of seafoam.

I am only selling this because I am moving to Puerto Rico and it will not fit in my suitcase.

This car is a great option for anyone looking for a car that you can rely on and that if you ever don’t want it you can sell again for basically the same that you paid for it.

Also if you don’t like the spoiler you can remove it with a wrench and cover the hole with epoxy and paint over it and you will never notice it was there.
