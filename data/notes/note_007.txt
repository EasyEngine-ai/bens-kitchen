Potato Pesto Goat Cheese Muffins

1.Bake medium potatoes then roll them in butter and coat them with salt/flour/cornstarch/baking powder.
2.Put them in muffin tray and use seasoning jar to smash them into like a pocket of potato
3.Bake them at high heat to crisp them up and then take them out.

4.Mix Pesto, Roasted Garlic, Goat Cheese, Greek Yogurt, Cottage Cheese and Feta together in a bowl then pull the now crispy potato pockets out of the oven and fill them with the mixture you just made. Put it back into the oven at 350 for 7 min

5.Mix Cherry Tomatoes, red onions, cucumbers, corn, cherry peppers, basil, olive oil, red wine vinegar and balsamic glaze in bowl. Sprinkle in crushed crushed ridged kettle chips

6.Take Potatoes out of the oven and out of the pan then put the tomato salad mixture on top of the Pesto and cheese mixture so it fills the potato Pocket completely
