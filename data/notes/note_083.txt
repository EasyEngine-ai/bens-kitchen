Bacon Onion and Roasted Garlic Jam

Bacon
Yellow onions
Garlic
Brown sugar
Apple cider vinegar
Burbon Maple syrup
Black pepper
Salt

How to make it:
Cook diced bacon until crispy, remove and set aside. In the bacon fat, cook diced onions low and slow until deeply caramelized (about 30–40 minutes). Add minced garlic and cook a couple more minutes. Stir in brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, and maple syrup. Add the bacon back in and simmer on low until it becomes thick and jammy (about 15–20 minutes). Season with salt and pepper. Let it cool slightly — it’ll thicken more as it rests.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
