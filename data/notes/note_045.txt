Asian Cucumber Salad 
	∙	English  cucumbers
             Cabbage 
	∙	Rice vinegar
	∙	Soy sauce
	∙	Toasted sesame oil
             Thai sweet chili 
             Crispy chili oil
	∙	Sugar or honey
	∙	Garlic, minced
            Red onion 
	∙	Fresh ginger, grated
	∙	Red pepper flakes
	∙	Sesame seeds
	∙	Green onions
	∙	Crushed peanuts (optional)


Slice cucumbers as thin as possible (mandoline works best). Whisk together rice vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar, garlic, ginger, and red pepper flakes until sugar dissolves. Toss cucumbers in the dressing. Refrigerate at least 10–15 min to let flavors meld. Top with sesame seeds, sliced green onions, and crushed peanuts before serving.
