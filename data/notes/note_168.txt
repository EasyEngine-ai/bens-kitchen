Classic Chicken Sandwich Sauces

Standard Sauce-

Mayonnaise
Pickle Juice or Lemon Juice
Salt & Pepper
Dill
(Option: Paprika or Rosemary)
Saute Garlic and Red Onions in Bacon Fat Until Slightly Crispy (Option: Add Red Bell Pepper or Poblano Pepper into Saute)
Chop up pickles into small chunks or us Dill Relish if lazy (Option: use banana peppers also) mix everything together
