Smoked and Candied BBQ Hotdog Burnt Ends

Beef Hotdogs or cheddar Kielbasas chopped into 1 1/2in slices 
Use Yellow mustard and honey as binder 
BBQ seasoning, brown sugar, jalapeño seasoning 
Smoke for a while then
Coat with devil spit bbq sauce and more brown sugar 
Then put in aluminum tin with butter and more sugar and Sweet Baby Ray and cook in the tin at 400 degrees for 30min to an hour
