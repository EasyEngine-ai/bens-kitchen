Sauces

Mango Habanero Sauce

This Mango Habanero Sauce is a vibrant blend of sweet and spicy, featuring luscious mango puree, fiery minced habaneros, and fragrant garlic. Finished with a squeeze of zesty lime juice and a touch of honey, each dip explodes with flavor. Perfect for adding a kick to grilled meats or as a bold dressing for fresh salads!

MIDDLE EASTERN & MEDITERRANEAN

Zhug (Yemeni Green Sauce) Chicken Sandwich

Yemeni fire meets fried chicken — zhug is a bright, spicy green chili sauce loaded with fresh cilantro, parsley, serrano peppers, and garlic that gets drizzled over crispy chicken. A fresh herb and feta slaw adds crunch, a spiced bacon jam and a zhug-spiked goat cheese spread load up the bottom bun. Pita chips for crunch and sumac-dusted red onions on top. Herby, spicy, and addictive.
